About Me

I am a Business Intelligence Analyst with a background in mathematics education and a strong focus on turning business data into clear decisions.

I do more than build dashboards. I investigate the problem behind the numbers, define the right KPIs, clean and model the data, and present insights that help leaders act faster. My projects cover retail, FMCG, real estate, customer experience, and e-commerce, using tools such as Power BI, SQL, Excel, Databricks, and PySpark.

What makes my work different is my ability to connect technical analysis with business action. Whether I am analysing revenue, customer behaviour, sales performance, profitability, or operational gaps, my goal is always the same: make the data easy to understand, easy to trust, and useful for decision-making.

I have built more than 15 dashboards and completed end-to-end analytics projects that combine data preparation, modelling, visualisation, and business recommendations. I am currently open to Business Intelligence Analyst opportunities where I can help organisations improve reporting, identify performance gaps, and make better decisions with reliable data.

My Projects

FMCG Sales Variance Analysis Dashboard

Built a sales variance and forecasting dashboard that compared actual sales against targets across months, territories, and sales representatives. The analysis showed that annual sales exceeded target by ₦46.6 million, while also revealing seasonal gaps and regional performance differences, helping leadership improve forecasting, territory planning, and early intervention.

Customer Experience & Satisfaction Performance Dashboard

Analysed customer survey data for a real estate company to identify the factors driving low satisfaction, weak trust, and poor referral rates across Abuja, Lagos, and Port Harcourt. The dashboard connected customer complaints, pricing transparency, agent professionalism, and communication quality to loyalty outcomes, helping management prioritise service improvements and customer retention actions.

Documentation View Dashboard

GlowMart Sales & Inventory Business Intelligence Dashboard

Developed a Power BI solution to resolve conflicting assumptions between the Sales and Inventory teams about product availability. By analysing revenue, profitability, regional demand, and product performance, the dashboard showed that inventory should be allocated based on actual demand rather than distributed equally, helping management reduce stock shortages and improve regional planning.

GlobalShop International Retail Analytics

Built an end-to-end retail analytics solution that transformed more than 60,000 historical sales records into trusted, decision-ready insights using Databricks and Power BI. The solution gave management clear visibility into product profitability, customer segments, revenue concentration, and country performance, supporting faster decisions on pricing, customer retention, product strategy, and market focus.
